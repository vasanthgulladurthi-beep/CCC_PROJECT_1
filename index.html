💡 Algorithm Disclaimer

The Greedy Algorithm makes the locally optimal choice at each stage with the hope of finding a global optimum. It is highly efficient (O(N log N) due to sorting).

However, it does not guarantee the mathematically perfect combination (unlike Dynamic Programming). It might skip a high-utility item if it's too expensive early on, potentially leaving budget unused that could have been better filled.
